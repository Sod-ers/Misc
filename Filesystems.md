```
FAT32
+ Compatible everywhere.
- Cannot handle files larger than 4GB.

exFAT
+ Compatible almost everywhere.
+ Can handle files larger than 4GB.
- Not as compatible as FAT32.

NTFS
+ Compatible with Windows.
- Not compatible with Mac & most hardware devices.
- Occasional compatibility problems with Linux (NTFS is proprietary & reverse engineered).

EXT4
+ Modern, stable, fast, journalized.
+ Supports Linux file permissions.
- Not compatible with Windows, Mac & most hardware devices.
  ```
